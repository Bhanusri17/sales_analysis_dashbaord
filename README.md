📊 FestMan Sales – Financial Analysis Report

An interactive Sales & Financial Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, orders, profit, profit margins, discounts, countries, business segments, and product performance.

📌 Project Overview

The FestMan Sales – Financial Analysis Report is a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project analyzes financial sales data and converts it into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important business metrics and allows users to analyze performance across different countries, business segments, products, discount bands, and time periods.

The project demonstrates the process of transforming business data into an interactive dashboard for data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Monitor order performance.
Analyze total profit and profitability.
Calculate and compare profit margins.
Analyze discounts across different discount bands.
Compare orders across countries.
Compare profit margins across countries.
Analyze profit margins across business segments.
Analyze product-level profitability.
Identify the top 3 products based on sales amount.
Analyze sales trends by year and month.
Present business information through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Support data-driven business decision-making.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualization
Power Query – Data preparation and transformation
DAX – Calculations, measures, and KPIs
Microsoft Excel – Source financial sales data
Data Visualization – Presenting business insights
Business Intelligence – Business performance analysis

🔄 Data Analytics Workflow
Raw Financial Sales Data → Data Import → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Data Modeling → DAX & KPI Computation → Data Visualization → Interactive Dashboard → Business Insights

📈 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard contains five major KPIs:

Sales

Displays the current sales amount and compares it with the previous year's sales performance.

Orders

Shows the current number of orders and provides a comparison with the previous year.

Profit

Displays the current profit and compares it with the previous year's profit.

Profit Margin

Shows the current profit margin and its comparison with the previous year.

Discount

Displays the current discount amount and compares it with the previous year's discount.

📊 Dashboard Visualizations
1. Orders by Country

A bar chart comparing the number of orders across countries including Canada, France, United States, Mexico, and Germany.

2. Profit Margin by Country

A comparison of profit margins across different countries to understand geographical differences in profitability.

3. % of Discount Offered by Discount Band

A donut chart showing the distribution of discounts across the Low, Medium, and High discount bands.

4. Profit Margin by Segment and Products

A detailed table showing profit margins for different business segments and their products, including Small Business, Midmarket, and Government.

5. Profit Margin by Segment

A bar chart comparing profit margins across different business segments.

6. Sales by Year and Month

A line chart showing sales trends over time and helping identify changes in monthly sales performance.

7. Top 3 Products by Sales Amount

A visualization highlighting the top three products based on sales amount.


🧹 Data Preparation

The financial sales dataset was prepared before creating the dashboard.

The data preparation process involved reviewing the imported dataset, preparing the required fields, transforming data where required, ensuring appropriate data types, and preparing fields required for calculations and visualizations.

Power Query was used as part of the data preparation process.
